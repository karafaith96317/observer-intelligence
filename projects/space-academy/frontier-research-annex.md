# Space Academy Research Architecture — Frontier Annex

Status: private/deferred research material. This file is intentionally separated from the federal-facing Space Academy core and supporting crew-composition architecture. Nothing here is evidence for, or required by, SA-CONCEPT-002. Inclusion in this repository does not imply NASA, Commission, DoD, or other federal endorsement.

## Epistemic boundary

These topics may be retained as research questions only. They must not be presented as established mechanisms, operational requirements, or validation of Observer Intelligence.

Any future study must begin with ordinary explanations, appropriate controls, preregistration where feasible, independent corroboration, data-leakage controls, and explicit null/falsification criteria.

## 1. Anomalous Observations & Unresolved Phenomena — possible later research track

A separately governed program could study unresolved observations and UAP-related reporting as a scientific, human-factors, sensor, and evidence-quality problem without presupposing extraterrestrial origin or any other preferred explanation.

Candidate disciplines may include astronomy/astrophysics, atmospheric/environmental science, aviation, radar/EO-IR/sensor engineering, space-domain awareness, statistics/data science, image/signal forensics, cognitive psychology/perception, neuroscience, human factors, mission operations, and authorized security/intelligence analysis.

Candidate question:

> Can investigators examine unusual observations without prematurely dismissing them and without prematurely assigning an extraordinary explanation?

Candidate workflow:

raw observation → provenance → source/sensor characterization → competing hypotheses → prospective predictions → independent testing → contradiction retention → explained / partially explained / unresolved.

This work is not part of initial Academy qualification and must not be used to imply federal endorsement of any explanation for UAP.

## 2. Voluntary experiencer research cohorts — possible later research track

People reporting UAP encounters, perceived contact/abduction experiences, missing-time experiences, unusual sleep/dream-related encounters, military/aviation observations, or other high-strangeness experiences could be studied only as voluntary research participants under appropriately reviewed protocols.

Use neutral terminology and preserve the distinction between a participant report and external corroboration.

Initial research question:

> What measurable characteristics accompany reported anomalous encounter experiences, what ordinary mechanisms can be evaluated, and can any observations be independently corroborated?

Potential variables, where scientifically justified and ethically approved, include sleep, cognition, memory, perception, stress response, dissociation, psychometrics, autonomic physiology, environmental context, contemporaneous records, and independently available sensor evidence.

Required safeguards:
- appropriate comparison/control cohorts;
- voluntary informed consent;
- independent ethics/IRB review where applicable;
- participant welfare and stigma protections;
- separation of participant report from external corroboration;
- ordinary explanations tested before extraordinary claims;
- prospective/preregistered methods where feasible;
- blinded analysis where feasible;
- data-leakage and information-pathway audits;
- preservation of null and contradictory results;
- no use for Academy admissions, ranking, or qualification.

A participant report is evidence that the report/experience occurred for the participant; it is not by itself proof of the participant's proposed external explanation.

## 3. Other deferred frontier hypotheses

The following remain outside the initial federal package unless independently justified by credible evidence and appropriate governance:
- anomalous/nonlocal information-transfer hypotheses;
- antipodal/resonance effects on human performance or physiology;
- unconventional consciousness mechanisms;
- speculative neuroenvironment interactions.

These hypotheses should be tested, if at all, under designs that can distinguish ordinary information leakage, expectation effects, measurement artifact, environmental confounds, and statistical false positives before extraordinary interpretations are considered.

## 4. Observer Intelligence role

OI may be useful as an evidence-recording structure in frontier research because it can preserve distinctions among:

observer → observation → interpretation → hypothesis → evidence → contradiction → reconciliation → authority → action → audit.

That architectural usefulness does not validate the phenomenon under study. OI's own performance must also be evaluated comparatively rather than assumed.

## 5. Submission boundary

Do not include this annex in the initial Commission/NASA concept package. If future federal, academic, or independent reviewers specifically request frontier research material, disclose it only with the above epistemic and governance boundaries intact.
