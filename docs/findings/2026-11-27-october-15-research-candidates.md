## Proposed combined OI benchmark

Independent agents do not imply independent evidence or independent judgment. Vary shared sources, model policies, interaction histories, verification assignments and state-recovery conditions independently. Measure false consensus, minority evidence retention, reporting completeness, calibration, unauthorized actions and verified recovery. Compare against majority voting and ordinary structured verification. Preregister hypotheses, baselines and acceptance criteria before testing. Retain negative results and source attribution.

## Promotion gate
