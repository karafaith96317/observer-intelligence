## Synthesis: hypothesis → measurement → falsification
- **H1 Authorization:** A pre-execution policy enforcement point blocks explicitly prohibited tool calls. Test vs identical conventional policy gate. This is not inherently novel.
- **H2 Evidence integrity:** With an external trusted checkpoint, changes to an earlier hash-chained audit record are detected; without one, rollback/deletion can escape detection.
- **H3 Source credibility:** Source reputation is not substituted for claim-level corroboration; measure false acceptance on conflicting claims.
- **H4 Environmental provenance:** Time, units, instruments and uncertainty can be preserved when ingesting publicly available planetary observations. No physiological causal claim.
- **H5 Physiological validity:** Candidate EEG/HRV/EDA features must be evaluated on external datasets with prespecified outcomes, consent, privacy safeguards and uncertainty; no identity, truthfulness or access decisions based on speculative proxies.
