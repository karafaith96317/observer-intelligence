## Research synthesis: OI / ERA-GOS
1. **Credibility is not truth:** retain provenance, independent corroboration, uncertainty and authorization separately.
2. **Biomarkers are not intentions:** EEG/MEG/HRV/EDA cannot be converted into claims about honesty, mental state or eligibility without validated use-specific evidence.
3. **Explanation is not enforcement:** an agent's stated reason for a tool call is not proof that the tool call is authorized.
4. **Forecasts are not outcomes:** preserve timestamps and uncertainty; update records without rewriting earlier forecasts.
5. **Evidence is not permission:** require policy authorization before execution; log both blocked and allowed attempts.
