## What looks strongest after the full pass

1. **Calendar recurrence is the cleanest non-transformed signal.** July 3 and March 8 are objective date clusters and do not depend on choosing a chart frame.
2. **The 24°–26° Aquarius corridor survives recalculation across different carriers and frames.** It is structurally interesting, but still post hoc.
3. **The ~16°45 Pisces and ~27°05 Cancer four-person carrier substitutions reproduce extremely closely.** These deserve preservation as fixed targets for future held-out cases.
4. **Kara–Russ Dailey has a very tight ordinary tropical Mars/Pluto conjunction (~0.023°).** This is more interpretable than many exotic-frame coincidences because it does not require a custom transformation.
5. **The focal family contains repeated Cancer/Capricorn, Virgo/Scorpio and 21°–26° structures**, but broad family-vs-nonfamily excess has not yet been demonstrated statistically.
6. **Extremely exact planet-centered matches are common once the search surface is enormous.** Therefore the existence of a 0.001° transformed match by itself is not strong evidence. The scientifically stronger move is to freeze the targets above and test new people/events without moving the goalposts.

## Current falsification rules

- Keep birth-time uncertainty explicit; no houses/angles for unknown times.
